What do “soft punt” and “hard punt” mean?

A punt is accepting a weak category to buy more help elsewhere. It is a trade, not free value.

Soft punt: you still care about the category, but you will not pay as much to protect it. Example: accept a guard who takes difficult shots if his assists and threes are worth that cost.

Hard punt: you give that category no extra credit when deciding what to bid. The category still affects your final standing. You might finish last, but that is not automatic.

Two-category punt: the same choice for two categories. Be careful: if both actually finish last, even first place in every other category only gives 74 out of 96 points in a 12-team league.

Choice B: how do you spread the $200?

What does a category win mean?

In your 12-team league, finishing first in assists gives 12 points; finishing last gives 1. Add the points from all eight categories.

You do not win by simply winning five categories against one opponent. Giving up one category costs points that the other seven must recover.

Basketball stats versus standings points

Basketball points are what a player scores in games. Standings points are what your team earns for its place in a category. A team with the most basketball points gets 12 standings points in that category; the other seven categories earn their own standings points.

The eight categories: field-goal percentage (shots made out of shots taken, excluding free throws); free-throw percentage; made three-pointers; basketball points; rebounds; assists; steals; and blocked shots.

Which contributions are hardest to replace?

Blocks: a smaller group of leading players supplies a large share. Prepare more than one way to buy this help, rather than depending on one favorite being affordable.

Passing and scoring: this pool has few strong alternatives outside the usual draft range. Leaving the whole job until late is risky.

Threes and steals: there are specialists further down the pool, but their other contributions may be weak. A lower-ranked specialist is not automatically available or a $1 bargain. Click through an example to inspect the whole player.
