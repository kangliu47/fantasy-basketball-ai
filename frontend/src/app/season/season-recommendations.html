How per-game fit works

All players use the same dated league priorities, scoring directions and selected rate source. Actuals sum completed-game statistics and divide by played games; percentages use combined makes and attempts. Minutes are context, not a second rate multiplier. Counts use current league 90th–10th percentile spans; shooting uses excess makes at your current team ratio, divided by current earned attempts and the percentage span. A missing or zero scale withholds complete fit. This index is not roto points, a weekly forecast or past fantasy contribution.

Priorities combine gains and protection: Balanced 1/1; Chase 1/0.25; Protect 0.35/1.75. They are heuristics. Equal current values provide immediate tie-breaking opportunities; missing neighbors supply no term. Early-season geometry can be fragile because credited opportunities differ.
