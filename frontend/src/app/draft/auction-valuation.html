How the baseline works

Category z-scores use the same declared population, with shooting impact weighted by attempts. The first player outside the modeled purchase set sets the replacement score. After reserving minimum bids, higher scores share the remaining room dollars.

Values use cents so the room reconciles exactly. Actual bids and personal ceilings use whole dollars. Players outside the modeled purchase set are unpriced, rather than worth zero.

This is a budgeting heuristic. Positions, lineup use, streaming and team assignments are not modeled. Values stay fixed as sales are recorded; the ledger updates your legal maximum.
