How the vector becomes a recommendation

Use the priority vector to screen net add/drop contributions in comparable units. Recalculate all category outcomes for the shortlist when a complete future baseline is available. Check participation and replacement sensitivity. Keep roster is always a baseline.

Fit index = Σ category priority × normalized (incoming − outgoing) contribution

This index is not roto points. Its scales, weights and source coverage are inspectable. Cosine alignment alone ignores the amount of improvement. Shooting uses makes and attempts; finite endpoint ratios are recomputed in the complete example.

Future target: expected roto points with the move − expected roto points keeping the roster

That target requires all-team future production, usable starts, participation uncertainty, hold duration and a policy after this decision window. Probability calibration must be tested. A seven-day conditional improvement does not establish a season-end gain.
