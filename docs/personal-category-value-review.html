Review rule and fixture checks

In the five-season view, a possible excess-outcome pattern needs at least three jointly eligible manager seasons, repeated above-baseline outcomes, CAP_CANDIDATE boundary evidence, a better-side boundary in at least two-thirds of eligible seasons, and hold cushions at least as large as the season/category median distinct-tier gap in at least two-thirds. Nearby historical gain needs repeated below-baseline outcomes, at least three seasons with a next better tier, and a normalized next-tier gap no larger than that season/category median normalized distinct-tier gap in at least two-thirds. The three-season view may show a recent pattern but never receives the stable possible-excess label.

Values are calculated from the exact displayed tiers. Ties remain one tier with an average rank. Positive affine rescaling changes neither normalized gaps nor labels; a zero P90–P10 spread leaves normalized gaps unavailable and withholds classification. Missing observations or reviewed manager assignments are exclusions, never zeroes.
