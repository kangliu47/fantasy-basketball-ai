What I am trying to learn

A small product experiment, made inspectable.

The project uses a real personal question to practice product discovery and engineering judgment—without confusing research ideas with committed product scope.

Outcome

Make better future draft decisions.

Use completed-season evidence to understand which differences are personal, which are league-wide, and which are too uncertain to act on.

Current questions
  • How has auction budget been allocated?
  • How do competitors differ?
  • Which category results persist?
  • Which patterns are league-wide?

Current experiment: compare historical auction concentration and category finishes as decision evidence.

Product heartbeat

One active focus, with uncertainty visible.

These are intentionally curated product directions, not automatically generated from commits or an unbounded backlog.

Now

Validate historical decision support

Make historical manager and category comparison clear enough to assess whether it is genuinely useful for draft preparation.

Next

Explore draft pricing questions

Investigate whether projections plus league-specific history can inform a maximum auction price.

Later

Live-season decisions

Consider waiver, FAAB and roster choices only when current-season inputs and a useful decision baseline exist.

Feedback requested now

Would this competitor comparison affect how you bid in a future auction?

Helpful feedback names the specific result that feels actionable, unclear, or unsupported by the evidence.

How the product evolved

Usability correction

Feature breadth made the personal journey harder to find.
