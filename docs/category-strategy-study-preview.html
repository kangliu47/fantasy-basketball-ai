How to read the chart and its evidence

Rank runs from worse to better, matching the existing My profile charts. Dots represent exact tied tiers; tied ranks are averages. The curve describes final standings. Integer point-gain steps change only your category total and rescore you against fixed opponents, including joining or passing a tie.

Gap summaries use the median within each season, then an equal-weight median across seasons. Top, middle and bottom use adjacent-tier rank midpoints: ≤4, >4 to ≤9, and >9. The common bar unit is each gap divided by that season’s P90–P10 league spread.
