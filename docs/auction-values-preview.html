How the baseline works

All selected categories use the same declared projection population. Counting contributions and volume-aware shooting impact are standardized, then summed. Players above the modeled replacement cutoff share the dollars left after minimum bids.

Active and bench spots determine purchases. IR is recorded separately and adds no purchases here. Positions, lineup usage and replacement production for missed games are not modeled. A deeper bench can change the cutoff; more IR alone does not change these allocations.

Values are a budgeting heuristic, not predicted sale prices or an optimized roster. The personal ceiling remains your decision. Values stay fixed during the auction; recorded sales only change the legal maximum.
