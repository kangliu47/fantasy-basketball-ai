stats are not used for injury fit or role calculations. The existing dated,
completed-game adapter remains the calculation source.

validated current NBA report links when accessible. Synthetic PDF checks pass;
real official PDF ingestion through the unavailable directory is not claimed.
PDF names remain original review evidence, not automatic ESPN identity matches.

season outlooks do not trigger injury events. Claim processing weekdays, cutoff,
timezone, future credited starts and transaction legality remain unverified;
