and outgoing starts and names the replacement. Balanced, Chase gains and Protect
leads change heuristic priorities without modifying actual scoring rules. Current
league geometry determines dated category shares/scales; complete saved projection
rates supply signed net contributions. All categories, shooting makes/attempts,
negative effects, keep 0 and bounded incoming-start sensitivity remain visible.
No positive covered move and insufficient evidence have distinct states. Search
and position filters retain missing-rate players as unranked entries.

Recent ESPN performance and dated news stay in the shared player inspector.

method and priority snapshot under existing revision checks. Revisit restores
assumptions and recomputes against current evidence. Unsaved reasons survive
ranking-mode/strategy changes and failures. Prior player-only notes remain valid.

## Limits

The index is an inspectable heuristic based on current, potentially unequal
credited opportunities. It is not a season-end forecast, a calibrated probability,
expected roto gain, optimal-roster proof or verified transaction recommendation.
Participation, clearance, lineup capacity and caps remain unverified. Missing
required evidence withholds complete fit rather than becoming zero. No new
provider fetch, background monitor, database writer or external roster action is
introduced by the overall-fit use case.
