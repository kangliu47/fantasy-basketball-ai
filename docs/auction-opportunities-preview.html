How to read this

The baseline stays fixed. A conditional stop depends on remaining money, available alternatives and a feasible balanced completion. Buy targets are upper aims, not minimum prices. ESPN pre-draft values are specific to their room setup; average salary has a separate sampling context.

The buy list excludes severe individual FG/FT contribution below −2 z. Counting-category weaknesses can be complemented. All-player and nomination views retain the exclusions for inspection. Bench candidates with no baseline stay unpriced. Old targets, old caps and practice results do not shape this view.

Review controls only. In the app, a verified finalized result updates the chart, availability and budget automatically. MCP and the UI read the same local state.
